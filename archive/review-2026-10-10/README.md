# Resonant dimer transport

A locally created pair in a fractional-hopping chain has a persistent late-time coincidence signal. Exactly at the binding resonance its velocity law has an algebraic unbounded tail; at every fixed stronger attraction the limiting velocity support is bounded. Local source and detector overlaps suppress the fastest events, and constant overlap with a fast eigenstate requires a growing relative preparation radius.

Read the [consolidated theorem and size corollary](THEOREM.md), [proof/contribution review](REVIEW.md), and [project handoff](PROJECT_HANDOFF.md). The [preceding transport proof](prior/FOLLOWUP.md) and [spectral pilot](prior/prior/PILOT.md) remain unchanged.

This is not a finite-time light cone, a finite-weight superballistic front, or a device advantage. The mathematical and experimental ingredients have explicit predecessors. The contribution to assess is the microscopic, source-resolved resonance contrast.
